How it works

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to search?

Yes. Create a free account with your name, phone and address. It takes a minute.

Which areas are covered?

Right now pharmacies inside Sylhet city: Zindabazar, Amberkhana, Bondor Bazar, Subidbazar, Tilagor, Uposhohor, Chowhatta and more.

How do I pay?

Pay the delivery person in cash when the medicine arrives.

Can I buy medicine that needs a prescription?

The pharmacy may ask to see the prescription at delivery and can reject the order without it.

How can my pharmacy join?

Register as a pharmacy with your shop name, license number and address, then add your stock.
